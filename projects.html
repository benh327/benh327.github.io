Golf Sphero Capstone Design

This project was for development of a custom software for a Sphero BOLT+ that allowed for measuring the greens conditions of a golf course. These parameters include three key metrics: Stimp rating (green speed), trueness, and smoothness. The software was developed in Python and utilized some of the same frameworks used by the Sphero Edu app, including the bleak library used for BLE (bluetooth Low Energy) communication. Through the BLE connection, we were able to handle all of the sensor data and timestamps so that we could model and predict the turf metrics. Data was collected primarily at the Auburn University Turfgrass Unit where a variety of testing surfaces are used for university research.
